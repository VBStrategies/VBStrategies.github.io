What each analysis contains

  1. One-page thesis: what the company does, which pillar it serves, why it wins, and what would prove the thesis wrong.
  2. Equity research report: sector analysis, business, financials, management and risks.
  3. Valuation: intrinsic value with the method that fits the business, cross-checked against trading comparables, with the model available to download.

Company names are examples for research, not recommendations.

Illustrative research, not investment advice. VB Europe Strategic is a model portfolio published for education, not a fund.
