About

Honestly, this started because I wanted a space of my own. I'm the kind of person who needs to actually build something to feel like I'm learning it — not just read about it and move on. So this site is where I put the work I do: valuations, models, notes from my studies. Writing things down and structuring them properly forces me to understand them, and that's really the core reason it exists.

There's a second reason too. I've often felt that I have all this time and interest in how the world of investing works, but I'd get stuck not knowing where to start, or I'd lose momentum before finishing something. Building this in the open is my way of pushing against that — turning ideas into finished work I can actually show, one piece at a time.

And I genuinely want the feedback. If someone learns something from what I share, that's great. But if someone disagrees, criticizes my analysis, or points out where I went wrong — that's just as valuable, because it shows me exactly where to improve.

My aim is to keep building a body of work that reflects how I think about investing — and to do it consistently, treating it as a process rather than a one-off. Each project, each note, is another piece of that. I focus on strategies I can realistically research and build with the tools I have at hand, which keeps the work grounded and something I can actually see through.

Concretely, I want to work in investment management, and asset management in particular. This site is part of how I'm working toward that: a place to demonstrate my skills and my thinking as I prepare to enter the job market. It's both a personal learning system and a portfolio — a way to show what I can do, not just say it. In the longer run, I also see this as laying the groundwork for something more ambitious — potentially building toward an active fund of my own one day.

Over time, I'd like this to become a genuine intersection of the things I care about: finance, continuous learning, and sharing work in a way that's useful to others.

I started out in the field of finance through hands-on experience: I worked as a Financial Manager at UniWave družstvo, where I was responsible for financial planning. I co-founded a small business focusing on real estate deal sourcing for private investors. Over time, I went on to work as a Junior Risk Manager at Winstor investiční společnost a.s., where I focused on risk assessment for qualified investor funds. For my bachelor's thesis, I chose a fundamental analysis and valuation of SoFi Technologies, Inc., which is where my interest in equity analysis really took shape.

I'm currently pursuing an MSc in Innovative Finance at the University of Liechtenstein. Alongside my studies, this site is where I present the work I produce as I go — valuation projects, financial models, and structured notes from what I'm learning.

My goal is to work in Investment management or Asset management. Building this is part of how I'm working toward that: a way to demonstrate what I can do and show my thinking as I prepare to enter the job market.
