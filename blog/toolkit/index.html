Every main way to value a company, when each one fits, and how to turn many valuations into one portfolio. One page per model, all built on the same template so they can be compared.

Three ways to value a company

RelativeWhat does the market pay for similar companies? Multiples such as EV/EBITDA and P/E.
IntrinsicWhat are the company's future cash flows worth today? DCF and its relatives.
Option-basedWhat is flexibility worth? Real options for pipelines and staged projects.

The three approaches can give very different answers. That is not a flaw: each one asks a different question, and the gap between them is information.
