Model portfolio · European equities

VB Europe Strategic

A long-only portfolio of European companies that serve the continent's push for economic and strategic self-sufficiency. Fundamental analysis decides which companies enter; statistical optimisation decides how much each one weighs.

Strategy
Thematic, long-only European equities
Approach
Fundamental selection, statistical weighting
Universe
EU/EEA, Switzerland, Norway (ex UK) · market cap ≥ €250m
Components
Five sector pillars and a private equity sleeve, each ≥ 10%
Benchmark
MSCI Europe ex UK Net Return (EUR)
Target
+2% a year over the benchmark, rolling three years
Status
Design · statute v1.0 (draft), October 2026
